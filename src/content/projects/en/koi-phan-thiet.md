# KOI Phan Thiết — Commercial Client Project

A commercial web development collaboration for KOI Phan Thiết, a destination dining, cafe, and koi pond attraction in Phan Thiet, Vietnam. The engagement spanned from February to May 2026, delivering an international multilingual presentation layer optimized for tourists and local patrons alike.

## Scope & Engineering Focus

- **Multilingual Architecture**: Designed a 5-language setup utilizing Polylang to serve visitors in Vietnamese, English, Russian, Korean, and Chinese.
- **Dynamic Dining Menus**: Implemented specialized catalog layouts displaying restaurant offerings, specialty beverages, and venue highlights with localized descriptions.
- **Core Web Vitals Optimization**: Structured asset delivery, responsive image loading, and caching strategies to ensure swift page loads across mobile and desktop devices.
- **Content Management Workflows**: Empowered venue management staff with streamlined editorial workflows for seasonal events and promotion updates.
